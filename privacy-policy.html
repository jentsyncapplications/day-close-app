Privacy Policy

Your business records matter. Learn how Day Close handles and protects the information you enter.

App: Day Close Effective Date: November 12, 2026 Last Updated: November 12, 2026

Day Close ("the App") is a business cash tracking and daily expense management application operated by Cassie J Digital ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains what information Day Close handles, how that information is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to users.

Privacy at a Glance

Day Close currently operates offline. Business information entered into the App is stored locally on the user's device. The current version does not require users to create an online account or synchronize their business records with our servers.

We do not receive the business cash records or expense information entered into the App through the current offline functionality.

1. Information We Handle

Day Close allows users to enter business-related information to maintain daily cash records and review business cash activity.

Depending on how the App is used, this information may include:

  • Business name entered in the business profile.
  • Opening cash amounts.
  • Closing cash amounts.
  • Expense amounts.
  • Expense categories.
  • Expense descriptions and notes.
  • Business dates and recorded transaction times.
  • Internal record identifiers.
  • Calculated daily summaries and performance information.

Users may enter business information relating to employees, suppliers, customers, or other individuals in optional descriptions or notes.

Users should avoid entering unnecessary personal, confidential, or sensitive information about other individuals into these fields.

2. How Information Is Used

Information entered into Day Close is used to provide the App's business recordkeeping features.

This includes:

  • Recording opening and closing cash.
  • Recording and organizing business expenses.
  • Calculating total recorded expenses.
  • Calculating estimated daily cash inflow.
  • Displaying positive, zero, or negative cash differences.
  • Generating daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly summaries.
  • Displaying charts showing changes in estimated cash inflow.
  • Displaying business performance indicators based on recorded figures.
  • Displaying the user's business name within the App.

These calculations and summaries are generated to help users understand and organize their recorded business cash activity.

3. Local Data Storage

The current version of Day Close stores business records locally on the user's device.

Users can access their recorded information without requiring an active internet connection.

Under the current offline design, business records are not uploaded to our servers or made remotely accessible to the developer.

We do not use the current offline functionality to sell, distribute, or share users' business cash records with advertisers or other third parties.

Important:

Locally stored information may be lost if the application is uninstalled, the device is reset, the device is damaged, or application data is otherwise deleted.

Depending on the device's Android backup settings and the App's backup configuration, some application data may also be included in device-level backups. Such backups are subject to the relevant device platform's settings and policies.

Users should maintain appropriate backups of important business records.

4. Business Records and Financial Information

Day Close is designed to help business owners maintain daily records of cash available at opening, expenses incurred during operations, and cash counted at closing.

The App may calculate estimated cash inflow by comparing opening cash, closing cash, and recorded expenses.

These figures are estimates based on the information entered by the user. They are not independently verified sales figures, confirmed revenue, accounting profit, or proof of financial misconduct.

The accuracy of the results depends on the completeness and accuracy of the information entered into the App.

5. Internet Connectivity and Future Synchronization

Day Close currently provides its core business recordkeeping features offline.

We may introduce optional online services in future versions, which may include cloud backup, synchronization across devices, or other online functionality.

If such features are introduced, the relevant data handling practices will be explained in an updated Privacy Policy.

Where required, users will receive appropriate information and choices before their information is transmitted to external servers.

Any future online feature will be subject to its applicable privacy disclosures, security measures, and data retention and deletion procedures.

6. Third-Party Services

The current offline functionality does not require users to connect a bank account, payment service, or external business management account.

The App's privacy practices also depend on any third-party software development kits, libraries, or services included in the distributed application.

Where applicable, any third-party service that collects or processes user or device information will be disclosed in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

Users should also review the privacy policies of relevant platform providers, including Google, where applicable.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Under the current offline design, we do not receive or routinely share the business records entered into Day Close.

We do not sell users' locally stored business records.

Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law or a valid legal process, to the extent such information is actually available to us.

8. Data Security

Day Close is designed to keep business records stored locally on the user's device.

We take reasonable steps in developing and maintaining the App to support the security of its functionality.

However, no application or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Users are encouraged to:

  • Protect their devices using a PIN, password, or biometric authentication.
  • Avoid allowing unauthorized individuals to access the App.
  • Keep their device operating system updated.
  • Maintain backups of important business records.
  • Avoid entering unnecessary confidential information in expense notes.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Business records are retained locally for as long as they remain stored in the App or on the device.

Users can manage their records through the available functionality within Day Close, including editing or deleting records where supported.

Because the current version does not maintain an online account or developer-controlled server-side database of business records, we cannot remotely retrieve or delete locally stored records.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data may remove locally stored information, subject to Android device and backup behavior.

10. Children's Privacy

Day Close is a business management and recordkeeping application intended for business owners and other individuals involved in business operations.

The App is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the current offline functionality.

If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected through a future online service without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to address the situation in accordance with applicable law.

11. International Users

Day Close is intended for business users in different countries and regions.

Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of the App and the business records they maintain comply with the laws and regulations applicable to their location.

Depending on your country or region, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information where applicable.

For information stored exclusively on your device, you can manage or delete the relevant records using the App's available functionality or your device's application settings.
