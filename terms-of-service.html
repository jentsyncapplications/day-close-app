These calculations may use opening cash, closing cash, and recorded expenses to help explain the movement of cash during a business day.

Estimated cash inflow is a calculated figure based on the information entered. It is not necessarily equal to confirmed sales, total business revenue, or accounting profit.

The calculation assumes that the relevant cash movements have been properly recorded. Missing transactions, incorrect entries, unrecorded cash withdrawals, additional cash contributions, refunds, or other cash movements may affect the result.

Day Close does not independently verify transactions, sales, expenses, or the physical amount of cash available in a business.

A positive or negative estimated cash inflow figure does not automatically establish business profitability, financial loss, fraud, theft, or employee misconduct.
